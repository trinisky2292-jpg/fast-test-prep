# Broward County (BCPS) Grade 6 Adopted Textbooks — Research Findings

**Student context:** Apollo Middle School, Hollywood, FL — Broward County Public Schools (BCPS).
**Research date:** 2026-06-02.
**Purpose:** Mirror the chapter/unit structure of Broward's adopted Grade 6 textbooks in a kids' study app.

**Method note:** `browardschools.com` and `fldoe.org` partially block scrapers and bury adoption lists in BEEP (login-gated) + PDF board agendas. Where a Broward-specific document couldn't be opened directly, findings rely on (a) Broward's own social-studies adoption PDF, (b) the FL state-adopted list these districts must choose from, (c) Miami-Dade's public adoption tables (M-DCPS publishes them openly — useful cross-reference since both districts pick from the same state list), and (d) publisher Florida program pages. Confidence is labeled per item.

| **Science** | M/J Comprehensive Science 1 | **Elevate Science Florida, Course 1 (Grade 6)** — see note: state's newest adoption is *Savvas Science Explorations Florida ©2025* | Savvas Learning Company | Elevate ©2019; Savvas Science Explorations ©2025 | MEDIUM (publisher = Savvas confirmed for FL; exact program edition Broward currently runs not 100% confirmed) |

> **Key caveat on transitions:** Florida re-adopts on subject cycles. As of mid-2025 the state newly approved **myPerspectives Florida ©2026** (ELA) and **Savvas Science Explorations Florida ©2025** (Science). Districts roll these in over 2025-26/2026-27. So a current Grade 6 student may be using either the prior edition or the brand-new one depending on rollout. The **publisher and program family are stable**; only the © year shifts. For app TOC purposes the unit/topic structure below holds across editions.

## 2. SCIENCE — M/J Comprehensive Science 1 (Grade 6)

- **Title (current/widely-used):** Elevate Science Florida, Course 1 (Grade 6)
- **Title (newest FL state adoption, rolling in 2025-26+):** Savvas Science Explorations Florida ©2025, Comprehensive Course 1 (Grade 6)
- **Publisher:** Savvas Learning Company (both)
- **Edition/Year:** Elevate ©2019; Science Explorations ©2025 (1st Ed)
- **Confidence:** MEDIUM. Publisher = Savvas is well-supported for FL middle science. Whether Broward is on legacy *Elevate* or the new *Science Explorations* depends on rollout timing. (Miami-Dade's 2024 committee recommended **Savvas Science Explorations Florida ©2025, Comprehensive Course 1-3** for 6–8 — strong signal this is the new statewide direction.)

### Table of Contents — Elevate Science Course 1 (Grade 6) Topics
Topic/Chapter structure (Savvas Elevate, Gr 6):
1. Introduction to Matter (describe/classify matter, measuring matter, changes in matter)
2. Solids, Liquids, and Gases
3. Energy
4. Thermal Energy (heat transfer)
5. Introduction to Earth's Systems
6. Weather in the Atmosphere
7. Minerals and Rocks in the Geosphere
8. Plate Tectonics
9. (Earth science continuation — Earth's surface processes)
10. Living Things in the Biosphere (ecosystems / matter & energy flow)

*Confidence on TOC: MEDIUM — assembled from the Elevate Course 1 student-edition/workbook chapter list. The new Science Explorations ©2025 covers the same M/J Comprehensive Science 1 standards (matter, energy, Earth systems, weather, geosphere/plate tectonics, ecosystems) so the app's structure maps cleanly either way.*

## 4. ELA / ENGLISH — M/J ELA Grade 6

- **Title:** myPerspectives Florida English Language Arts, Grade 6
- **Publisher:** Savvas Learning Company
- **Edition/Year:** ©2022 (current) → ©2026 (new statewide adoption, FDOE-approved Aug 2025)
- **Confidence:** MEDIUM-HIGH. myPerspectives Florida is FDOE statewide-approved for Grades 6–12 ELA (covers 100% of B.E.S.T. ELA standards). Miami-Dade's 2025 ELA committee selected **"myPerspectives Florida ELA c2026, Grades 6-8" (Savvas)**. Broward historically uses myPerspectives for 6–12; a competing option some FL districts weigh is HMH *Into Literature*. **For Broward specifically, an official current-year board confirmation was not directly opened** — flagged MEDIUM-HIGH, not absolute.

### Table of Contents — Grade 6 Units
1. Unit 1 — **Childhood** (EQ: What are some of the challenges and triumphs of growing up?)
2. Unit 2 — **Animal Allies** (EQ: What is the connection between humans and animals?)
3. Unit 3 — **Modern Technology** (EQ: How does technology affect our lives?)
4. Unit 4 — **Imagination** (EQ: Where can imagination lead?)
5. Unit 5 — **Exploration** (EQ: What drives people to explore?)

*Each unit follows the myPerspectives structure: Whole-Class Learning → Small-Group Learning → Independent Learning → Performance-Based Assessment. Confidence on TOC: HIGH for unit titles/EQs.*

**Sources:** [Savvas myPerspectives Florida](https://www.savvas.com/florida/solutions/literacy/solutions-for-florida/myperspectives-florida) · [FDOE approves myPerspectives FL statewide (PRNewswire, Aug 2025)](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/florida-department-of-education-approves-savvas-learning-companys-myperspectives-florida-for-grades-6-12-ela-instruction-statewide-302531065.html) · [M-DCPS ELA Adoption 2025 preview table](https://innovationschoolchoice.com/instructional-materials/ela-adoption-2025/textbook-preview/)
